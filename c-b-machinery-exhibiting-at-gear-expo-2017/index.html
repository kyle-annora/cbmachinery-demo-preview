C&B Machinery Exhibiting at Gear Expo 2017

C&B announced its participation in Gear Expo 2017 at the Greater Columbus Convention Center in Columbus, Ohio, October 24–26. The company invited visitors to booth 1101 to discuss double-disc grinding technology and production applications.

Organized by the American Gear Manufacturers Association, the event brought together suppliers covering gear manufacturing, tooling, inspection, heat treatment and related processes. C&B’s exhibit focused on flexible grinding designs and application experience. This article is an archive of the 2017 event.
