# Customers and Partners — official C&B logo assets

Source page: https://www.cbmachinery.com/ · section “Customers and Partners”. Verified 2026-10-28.

These are the ten organizations and logo versions displayed in that section of the official website. The demo repeats that heading and list without making a more specific claim about the nature, recency or scope of any relationship. FCA remains the historical name/logo published by C&B; it is not relabeled as Stellantis. This section does not claim a current endorsement, quantified result or current contract.

The downloaded image bytes are preserved. Two image endpoints named `.webp` actually return JPEG data; their local filenames use `.jpg` to match that format. The website applies a grayscale CSS filter and conservative optical sizing for a quiet silver/white band. No logos are redrawn or cropped, no carousel, card borders or extra CTA are added.

Placement: after the homepage Explorer and before the machine-family section. Desktop uses two rows of five marks; tablet and mobile use a two-column static grid. Each image has its full organization name as alt text.
