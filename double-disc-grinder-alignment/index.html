Double Disc Grinder Alignment

C&B’s alignment overview follows the relationships between the base, spindles, dresser and part-handling tooling. A level, stable base comes first; spindle axes and faces are then referenced before the dresser and tooling are indicated to them.

The article describes spindle “zeroing,” dresser alignment and the position of carriers, fixtures and guides. Procedures differ between machine designs, and spindle adjustments are not presented as the first response to every problem.

After these relationships are established, process-specific head settings may be applied for shear or plunge grinding. Qualified service personnel should use the correct machine documentation. For recurring flatness, parallelism or finish issues, C&B offers technical assessment.
