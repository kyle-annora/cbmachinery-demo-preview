Does My Spindle Need a Rebuild?

Vibration, unusual noise and part-quality changes can indicate spindle trouble, but other assemblies can produce similar symptoms. C&B’s guide covers damaged boots, lubrication delivery, coolant-contaminated grease, spindle deflection and backlash. It also points to outboard supports and rotary unions as possible sources.

The original procedure begins with shutdown and lockout before inspection. Diagnostic running and disassembly should follow the machine’s procedures and be handled by qualified personnel; observations alone do not establish the failed component.

Scheduled maintenance helps detect wear, while a suspected fault may require teardown, inspection or specialist assessment. The linked service process explains C&B’s rebuild, balancing and test-stand work.
