Non-Coplanar Connecting Rod Grinding System

An updated DG2H-SA/30 cell supplied to Linamar’s Eston Manufacturing division processed a 1.8L connecting rod with a 3.8 mm symmetrical pin-end reduction. Total removal was 5 mm at the pin and 1 mm at the crank.

A two-stage cycle first roughed the pin end, then finished both ends together. It replaced three operations across two machines and reportedly exceeded the customer’s 1.67 Ppk requirements for size, parallelism and flatness.

Tooling allowed coplanar, stepped, pin-only and crank-only work with simple changeovers. The article also references the original Volt system and models using wheels up to 42 inches for larger diesel rods. Results are application-specific.
