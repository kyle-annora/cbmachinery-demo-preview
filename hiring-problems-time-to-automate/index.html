Hiring Problems…Time to Automate!

A push-rod-seat application moved from labor-intensive Blanchard grinding to a custom C&B single-disc machine with automatic loading and unloading.

The previous process produced 500 parts/hour and required a dedicated operator. The reported replacement produced 3,600 parts/hour while using 20% of the operator’s time.

This case illustrates the relationship between grinding method, handling automation and operator workload. The rates describe that specific part and cell.
