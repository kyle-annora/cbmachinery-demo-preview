Employee Spotlight – Craig Attard

C&B profiled Craig Attard as Purchasing / Spare Parts Manager in June 2023. He knew the company through a previous employer and described its experienced team as part of the attraction.

Attard began his career in assembly at Wheel Dressing Incorporated, where he worked for 22 years. He identified the new clamp-bore machine project as a particularly valuable experience, including the project-management work involved.
