Double Disc Grinder Alignment Issues

Alignment problems may appear as unstable size, poor parallelism or flatness, uneven grind patterns, localized burns or inconsistent finish. C&B’s article describes how wear, adjustments and maintenance affect the relationship between machine elements.

Areas to investigate include spindle feeds and heads, bearings, the dresser, entrance/exit guides and the tooling or feed mechanism. Their relative positions matter as much as the individual components.

The article cites application-dependent part tolerances of 0.0001–0.0005 inch to illustrate why alignment matters. It is a technical overview, not a substitute for the particular machine’s setup procedure. C&B service can evaluate persistent quality problems.
