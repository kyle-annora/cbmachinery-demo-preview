THE APPLICATION

A process defined
by your part.

Double-disc grinding uses the faces of two grinding wheels to remove material from opposing part surfaces. Machine configuration, handling and the grinding approach depend on the application.

For ring gears, that discussion can include finished thickness, flatness, parallelism, surface finish, material condition and production needs.

This application focuses on the ring gear’s faces. Gear-tooth geometry and tooth grinding are a separate process discussion.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Do I need to choose a machine first?

No. Start with your part and the result you need. The guided request collects the details for an engineering discussion.

What if my specifications are incomplete?

Share what you know. Material, dimensions, precision requirements or production targets can remain open for discussion. Historical case values are not applied to your project.

Can I include a drawing?

You can select a drawing or photo in the guided demo. This local concept records the file name and size; the file itself is not uploaded or sent to C&B.

Does the request give me a confirmed price?

The result is an application summary for review. Machine selection, achievable performance, delivery timing and pricing require a separate conversation with C&B.
