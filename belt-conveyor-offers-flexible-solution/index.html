Belt Conveyor Offers Flexible Solution

C&B replaced a steel gear conveyor and elevator with a dual side-belt conveyor. Handwheel adjustment allowed the conveyor to handle different gear sizes.

The design reduced part-to-part contact and could convey uphill, downhill or on a level path. The reported project reduced downtime, damaged or lost parts and changeover time; the customer subsequently set gear-production records.

The original article gives no numerical production figure. The example is a basis for discussing part handling and transfer requirements.
