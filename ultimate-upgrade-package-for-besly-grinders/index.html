Ultimate Upgrade Package for Besly Grinders!

C&B described retrofit packages for Besly DH6 and V-Series grinders, including spindle-head ball-screw infeed, replacement controls and panels, and a mobile touchscreen operator station. The work could be performed in the customer’s plant while retaining the base, tooling and fixtures.

The package added automatic dressing, feed precision, fault messaging and guided operator prompts. The article cited a possible four-minute dressing interval from good part to good part.

Historical commercial examples described approximately 85% remanufacturing, less than half a new machine’s cost and downtime as short as one week. These are the dated article’s examples; scope, pricing and installation timing require a current project review.
