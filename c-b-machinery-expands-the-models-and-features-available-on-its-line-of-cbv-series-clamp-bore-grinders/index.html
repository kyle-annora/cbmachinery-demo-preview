C&B Machinery Expands the Models and Features Available on Its Line of CBV Series Clamp Bore Grinders.

C&B expanded its CBV clamp-bore range with single-spindle, double-spindle and rotary-index arrangements. Single-spindle models addressed lower-volume work; dual stations allowed different workholding at each face; rotary-index configurations targeted greater throughput.

A higher-speed spindle option supported CBN wheels. Handling choices included manual loading, robots, gantries with part flipping, and in-process gauging with stock/runout pre-checks. The gauge fixture allowed tool-free adjustment.

Software controlled stock removal by face and part orientation at entry and exit. Part-type and load error-proofing could also be included. This announcement records the options described in August 2018.
