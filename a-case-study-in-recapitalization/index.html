A Case Study in Recapitalization

C&B retooled and requalified grinding cells moving from Silao, Mexico, to St. Catherines, Ontario. The contract covered three Mexican machines and an additional machine from Michigan. The cells included gantry handling, eddy-current checks, error-proofing and post-process gauging.

Requirements included five part configurations per machine, a maximum 30-minute changeover and compliance with Canadian CSA/PSR requirements. The source specifies three components per 18-second cycle and a production requirement written as “600 parts per”; the period is omitted in the original.

C&B documented the incoming assemblies, developed tooling and replacement drawings, coordinated automation and gauging suppliers, rebuilt worn spindle heads and debugged the complete cells. Testing included eight continuous hours before grinding optimization. The article reports delivery at approximately 30% of a new machine’s cost; this is a historical project result, not a current quotation.
