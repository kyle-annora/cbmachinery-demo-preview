About C&B Machinery

C&B Machinery has a team of engineers, machine builders, machinists and service technicians that not only understand grinding, they have a passion for it. Bring us your grinding questions and we will find you the solutions for your grinding needs.

C&B specializes in the design and build of New and Re-Engineered Production Double & Single Disc, Horizontal & Vertical Spindle, Super Finishing Clamp Bore, Park & Grind, and Special Application Grinders.

We work with clients to customize hardware and control retrofits, retools, and sub-assembly repairs. Need a machine? We maintain an inventory of re-engineered and used grinding machines. You don't want a machine or are not ready to purchase? We offer prototype and production grinding services.

Our Spare Parts and Tooling Department can work with you to procure and ship your spare or replacement parts, as well as, create a Preventative Maintenance Plan for your in-house grinding machines. Let our Service Technicians worry about your grinding machine maintenance.

In-House Capabilities

  • Manufacturing of High Precision Double Disc and Super Finishing Clamp Bore Grinding Machines
  • Techinical Service for grinding machines: at your facility or ours.
  • Rebuild, re-rengineer and/or retool existing grinding machines. We specialize in Besly, Gardner, Giustina, Mattison, and more.
  • Spindle rebuilds and core exchange programs to minimize machine downtime.
  • Machine controls system upgrades with Allen Bradley, Siemens, Fanuc, or to customer specifications.
  • Hand Scraping of machine ways on any type of machine.
  • Production grinding for prototypes, pre-production parts, manufacturing overflow, and gap grinding.
