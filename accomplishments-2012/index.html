C&B Machinery Accomplishments 2012

C&B’s 2012 review highlighted connecting-rod cells for General Motors, Linamar and Federal-Mogul. Each combined multiple grinding operations in one loading sequence.

For GM, the DG-2H-RE reciprocator completed loading, grinding, rinsing, blowing off and unloading in 21.8 seconds (165 parts/hour). Stock removal was 1.715 mm per crank face and 0.3 mm at the pin.

The Linamar flex cell combined a rough step cut with finish grinding: 4.9 mm pin reduction, 1.75 crank-end removal as written in the source, and 39.6 seconds (66 parts/hour). The source does not specify the unit for the 1.75 figure.

Federal-Mogul’s reciprocator removed 2.3 mm per crank face and 0.3 mm per pin face in an 18-second floor cycle (200 parts/hour), including handling.
