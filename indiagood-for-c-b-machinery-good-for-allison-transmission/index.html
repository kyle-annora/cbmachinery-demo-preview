India…Good for C&B Machinery, Good for Allison Transmission

Allison Transmission ordered a flexible double-disc cell for its Chennai facility to grind pinions for 1000- and 2000-series transmissions. The initial configuration covered four pinions, with scope for eleven more existing parts.

C&B integrated the grinder, automation, gauging, coolant system and bench gauges. Its rotary-plunge cycle kept wheels parallel and concentric, with measurement feedback for every component. Previously supplied cells exceeded 2.0 Ppk at an overall-length tolerance of ±0.030 mm and 0.026 mm parallelism.

The reported system could change between parts in as little as three minutes. This dated program illustrates flexible tooling and integrated process ownership; its results apply to the described application.
