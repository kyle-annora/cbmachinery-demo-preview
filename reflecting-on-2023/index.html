Reflecting on 2023

C&B’s 2023 review highlighted the launch and shipment of multiple CBVR-2F-2S clamp-bore machines, developed with customer input. The company reported repeat demand for the new design.

Community activities included two Job Shadow Days and National Manufacturing Day, coordinated with the Livingston Educational Services Agency. These gave high-school students a view of manufacturing careers and specific shop roles.

The year-end article recognized customers, vendors and business partners for their contributions to these projects and activities.
