C&B Secures Three Major Orders Across Key Industries

C&B announced three orders spanning automotive and aerospace applications.

The automotive transmission program covered six new dual-spindle vertical clamp-bore machines with flexible Fanuc robot handling, replacing competitor equipment. An aerospace Tier 1 order specified a horizontal Park & Grind machine for large round braking-system components.

A third program involved mechanical and electrical retrofit of a customer-supplied Besly horizontal through-feed grinder for an automotive Tier 1 supplier. It combined updated controls and components with the retained machine asset. These are the projects announced on August 6, 2025.
