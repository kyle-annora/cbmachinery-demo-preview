Employee Spotlight – Bob Waddell

The September 2023 profile introduced Bob Waddell as a machine builder with more than 17 years at C&B. He began machine building in 1979 and joined C&B after approaching its Livonia operation about available work.

Waddell described each new build as a fresh technical challenge. He also discussed more than 30 years of experience with robots and the need for collaboration among builders, programmers and controls engineers.

The full interview includes personal recollections. The role and tenure reflect the date of the original profile.
