Double Disc Grinding Services

The Challenge

Your machine is down, you are awaiting a new machine, you are going through a retool on your current machine, you have an order for a small batch of parts that are not able to be run on your machine..... and bottom line is: YOU NEED PARTS GROUND!

The Solution

We are here to help! We can provide PPAP verification for your parts and grind them for you. We will check part thickness and parallelism using a surface plate and indicator. This inspection will be documented at a predetermined frequency. Each part will be blown off via air to remove the majority of coolant/water. Parts will be lightly coated with a rust inhibitor, if required, and hand packaged into customer supplied dunnage. We always handle parts carefully to ensure no damage or nicks to the final product.
