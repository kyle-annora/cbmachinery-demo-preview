Model & interpretation

Apollo 11 latch assembly

Twenty original components from Andrew Barth’s Project Egress hatch model, published by the Smithsonian Institution. Source geometry and assembled positions are retained.

The silver, dark and bright finishes are presentation choices. The source labels these components as brushed steel; this study does not certify their physical materials.

Exploded movement illustrates component relationships. It is not an operating or service procedure. This focused review does not represent the full hatch’s performance.
