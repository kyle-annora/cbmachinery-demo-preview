Get Your Grind On – C&B Grinding School

C&B introduced an internal Grinding School to cross-train shop employees. The program aimed to broaden knowledge across roles, strengthen teamwork and make the organization less dependent on a single person’s specialist skills.

The article connects cross-training with continuity when employees change roles or leave, improved problem solving and more consistent support for customers. It describes an employee-development program rather than a public course registration offer.
