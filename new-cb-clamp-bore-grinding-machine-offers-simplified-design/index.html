New C&B Clamp Bore Grinding Machine Offers Simplified Design

C&B announced the redesigned CBVR-2F-2S clamp-bore face grinder after about a year of development involving sales, engineering, service engineers and customers. The model addresses close squareness and perpendicularity requirements, including EV drive gears.

The stated design goals included a simpler arrangement, shorter cycle times, lower cost, less downtime, fewer spare parts, reduced swarf buildup and better maintenance access.

The article presents the completed redesign and customer input behind it. It does not publish a universal cycle-time or tolerance guarantee; those depend on the application.
