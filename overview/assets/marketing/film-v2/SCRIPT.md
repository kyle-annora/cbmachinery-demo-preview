# C&B Machinery — Precision starts with your part

Version 2 direction · October 29, 2026 · 64 seconds · 1920 × 1080 · 30 fps

**Status: script and keyframe direction for production. This is not a completed replacement film.** The existing 55-second film remains a separate, earlier asset. This script supersedes its creative direction for the next film; it does not replace its source timeline or captions.

## The film's job

Give a manufacturing buyer a reason to begin a useful conversation with C&B. Make the manufacturer and equipment tangible, help the buyer understand the relationship to their part, then show how that understanding can continue into a brief and a proposal conversation.

The emotional progression is **recognition → understanding → collaboration**. The machine and C&B identity carry the opening. A real part gives the technical explanation a purpose. The ending makes the next action easy.

This is a C&B-facing customer film, not an Annora capabilities pitch. Search rankings, asset production, conversion strategy and platform architecture stay outside the narration. The proposed website experience is identified where it appears.

## What changes from the earlier film

- Open on the real branded equipment and return to it at the end. The manufacturer is visible before the buyer sees any form.
- Change composition deliberately: full equipment, large part, component focus, process principle, then a readable project. Avoid one small ring beside successive paragraphs for the entire film.
- Give each shot one message. Let motion reveal a relationship; keep type still long enough to read.
- Show collaboration after the inquiry: the same project moves to a proposal and an explicit discussion or revision request. A sent form is not the end of the story.
- End on one action, **Request a Quote**, with a clean eight-second closing frame. Do not add a second button or a mock success celebration.
