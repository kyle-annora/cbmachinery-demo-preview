COLOR ROLES / RECOMMENDED DIRECTION

Ink-burgundy canvas, burgundy actions, champagne emphasis.

Near-black burgundy extends C&B’s brand color. Equipment keeps its original colors, burgundy buttons lead to inquiries, and champagne highlights only keywords and the current step.

ACTION / BURGUNDY

#741D35 · Use #8F2D46 on dark backgrounds

Primary inquiry buttons use burgundy with white text. The dark-background version is slightly lighter, with a fine border to maintain its outline.

CANVAS / Ink burgundy

#21191C

Copy, equipment and explanations share one continuous dark canvas. Overlays and hotspots use the same color family.

EMPHASIS / CHAMPAGNE

#E8D8AF

Use only for headline keywords, the current step and selected hotspots, with a role distinct from inquiry buttons.

Both backgrounds use the same images, copy and interactions. Switching preserves the current exploration step; white reading areas and inquiry forms retain the established brand colors.
