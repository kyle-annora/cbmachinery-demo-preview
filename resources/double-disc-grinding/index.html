Begin with the two faces.

Double-disc grinding works with the faces of two grinding wheels on opposing surfaces of a part. This is different from using the wheel’s outside diameter as in cylindrical grinding.

The starting discussion includes the material to remove and the finished relationship between those surfaces.

Define what a good part means.

Finished thickness, flatness, parallelism and surface finish each describe a different part of the requirement. Bring the drawing and inspection context so engineering can review them together.

Published application examples provide context. Their values are specific to those cases and should not become default requirements for a new part.

The process includes what happens around the wheels.

Loading, part support, transfer and unloading belong in the same discussion. Orientation, geometry and production needs affect how the workpiece is presented.

Use the Machine Explorer to see these relationships, then bring your actual conditions into the inquiry.
