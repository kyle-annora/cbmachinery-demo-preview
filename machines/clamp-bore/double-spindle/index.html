Double Spindle Clamp Bore

Specifications:

  • Cast machine base
  • Vertical spindle axis orientation
  • Cup or ring disc grinding wheels
  • Variable rotations per minute
  • Servo drive feed system
  • In-process gauging
  • Feed rates: 1 – 30,000 mm/min
  • Position accuracy: .001 mm
  • AC motor with VF drive for rotation
  • Pneumatic clamping
  • 100 mm maximum workpiece diameter
  • 80 mm maximum workpiece length

Masterfully Engineered

A dual spindle clamp bore grinding machine is fully automated and the entire process is contained by guarding. Pick and place devices transfer parts between spindles and also to the reject/SPC chutes if needed.
