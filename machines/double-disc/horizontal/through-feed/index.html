Through Feed Tooling

Specifications

  • Cast machine base
  • Horizontal spindle orientation
  • 30, 36 & 42-inch diameter grinding wheels
  • 3.75-inch grinding wheel width
  • 3900-6500 surface feet per minute
  • Spindle motor horse-power varies based on wheel size (20-100 HP)
  • Quill feed spindle type
  • Precision ball screw wheel feed
  • Automatic dress cycle
  • Typically the highest part output tooling option
  • Grinding Wheels: Stationary
  • Type of Grind: Shearing or progressive grinding as component enters wheels
  • Head Settings: Closed or open in front depending on part configuration
  • Also Known as Thru-Feed or Belt-Feed Double Disc Grinders
