MACHINES

Rotary Carrier Tooling

Specifications

  • Cast machine base
  • Horizontal spindle orientation
  • 30, 36 & 42-inch diameter grinding wheels
  • 3.75-inch grinding wheel width
  • 3900-6500 surface feet per minute
  • Spindle motor horse-power varies based on wheel size (20-100 HP)
  • Quill feed spindle type
  • Precision ball screw wheel feed
  • Servo driven rotary carrier tooling
  • Automatic dress cycle

Conventional

  • Up to 2000 parts per hour
  • Stationary grinding wheels
  • Type of Grind: Shearing of 50-70% of stock as component enters wheels
  • Head Settings: Offset, tilted at top (or bottom) & closed in front

Rotary Plunge

  • One piece ground at a time
  • Grinding wheels infeed every cycle
  • Type of Grind: Plunge feed grind
  • Head Settings: Parallel
