Giustina double-disc rebuilds

C&B Giustina R242

C&B re-engineered this machine to face grind pinion gears at a cycle time of 3 seconds per part. This machine grinds the pinion gears that have a tight size and runout tolerance while meeting the surface finish requirement needed for the gear.

C&B Giustina R242

C&B re-engineered this machine to provide the flexibility to grind a wide range of part sizes and types. C&B equipped the machine with our quick change over style tooling to reduce downtime. The Servo axis control and automatic lubrication systems provide endless opportunities.
