Job Shadow Day – March 2023

On March 8, 2023, C&B hosted three students through the Livingston Educational Service Agency and its Career Development Center: two from Brighton High School and one from Hartland High School.

The students were enrolled in manufacturing education through the Livingston Career & Technical Education Center. Their visit included hands-on scraping, an introduction to grinding, and conversations with machinists, engineers and other employees.

The April article documented this Job Shadow Day visit and C&B’s continuing involvement with local career education.
