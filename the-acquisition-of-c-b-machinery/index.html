The Acquisition of C&B Machinery

On August 17, 2020, Orbitform and its investor group acquired C&B Machinery. The announcement said C&B would remain a separate entity with its staff and facility, supported by Orbitform’s manufacturing resources.

The acquisition brought together C&B’s disc-grinding experience and Orbitform’s forming and fastening capabilities. Bill Tulpa was introduced as C&B president in the announcement. This article records the transaction and leadership information as published in 2020.
