New Design “Clamp Bore” Makes Huge Impact!

General Motors selected C&B’s redesigned clamp-bore microfinishing machines to improve perpendicularity between gear faces and bores and reduce transmission noise. Parts were located on the bore, with each face pre-checked and ground sequentially.

The equipment combined vertical wheelheads and workheads, digitally controlled axes, programmable wheel speed, variable-speed workdrives, superabrasive compatibility and in-process gauging. Pneumatic or hydraulic clamping and customer-specific automation were available.

The release listed face, OD, groove and multi-step configurations. Siemens was the standard control; Allen-Bradley, Fanuc, Square-D and other options were also named. Claimed footprint and cost benefits belong to this 2012 design announcement.
