C&B Machinery to Grind Connecting Rods for the Chevy Volt

GM ordered two C&B double-disc systems to finish both coplanar and non-coplanar connecting rods for the Chevy Volt’s 1.4L auxiliary engine. On the turbo rod, the crank end was 1.2 mm narrower than the pin end.

Both ends were ground together: 1.8 mm stock removal at the crank and 0.6 mm at the pin, with output around 200 parts/hour. Reported results included flatness/parallelism averaging within 4 microns, 1-micron repeatability, size repeatability within 3 microns and a 6-micron step range.

A robot unloaded parts, verified orientation by camera, presented every part to a gauge and transferred it onward. The cell eliminated separate stepped-rod grinding operations and used no hydraulics or disposable filtration media. This dated report describes the Volt application, not a general machine guarantee.
