25 Years of Dedication!

This 2012 profile looked ahead to Jeff Allen’s 25th anniversary with C&B in January 2013. He began producing technical drawings for founder Joe Parker before joining as a full-time engineer, following quality-control work at R&B Manufacturing.

The profile traces Allen’s interest in drawing and mechanics, studies at Lawrence Technological University and experience seeing his machine designs built, tested and accepted. It also describes the company’s team events and close working relationships. The roles and anniversary are presented as they stood when the profile was published.
