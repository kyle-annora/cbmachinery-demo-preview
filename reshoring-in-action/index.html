Reshoring In Action

C&B described a 30-inch double-disc grinder being moved from an overseas site to a Michigan transmission program. The existing asset would be retooled, reautomated and updated for its new production setting.

The announced work included closed-loop automatic gauging, replacement of complex handling with a standardized Fanuc robot, a smaller pump-back system and tooling for the customer’s full part family.

Using common automation and tooling aligned the cell with equipment already used at the receiving plant. This article describes the reshoring project announced in February 2025.
