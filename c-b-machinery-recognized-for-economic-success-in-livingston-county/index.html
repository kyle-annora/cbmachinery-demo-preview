C & B Machinery Recognized for Economic Success in Livingston County

C&B Machinery was included in the Livingston County economic-success recognition reported by the Economic Development Council of Livingston County and Ann Arbor SPARK. The annual meeting highlighted investment, business leadership and regional collaboration.

The linked report also covered recognition for local education, manufacturing and transport organizations. C&B’s original post points to the November 2016 Business Wire announcement for the complete award context.
