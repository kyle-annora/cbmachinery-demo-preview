What is Double Disc Grinding?

Double-disc grinding removes stock from two opposing part faces using the faces of abrasive wheels. It is used where flatness, parallelism and repeatable thickness matter.

Suitability depends on geometry: the areas of the opposing faces, part size relative to wheel size, the relationship between length or diameter and width, and the thickness features being ground. Tooling, material and removal allowance also influence output.

The original overview notes a very wide production range—from a few parts/hour to over 40,000—across different applications. That range is not a rating for one machine. The accompanying tooling video explains several ways parts can move through the process.
