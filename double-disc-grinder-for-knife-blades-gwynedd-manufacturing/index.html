Double Disc Grinder for Knife Blades – Gwynedd Manufacturing

Gwynedd Manufacturing in North Wales, Pennsylvania, selected a 36-inch C&B swing-arm double-disc grinder for Abraham & Moses knife blades. Steel and powdered-metal blades up to 11 inches long required rough and finish grinding, with other operations between those stages.

The reported rough-grind size tolerance was ±0.0015 inch; finish size tolerance was ±0.001 inch. Both stages specified 0.003-inch parallelism and 32 Ra surface finish. Stock removal was 0.045–0.050 inch from raw stock to rough size, followed by 0.004 inch to finish.

The customer planned additional part families on the grinder. Swing-arm tooling supported changeovers between these geometries. Values are preserved as published for this specific knife-blade project.
