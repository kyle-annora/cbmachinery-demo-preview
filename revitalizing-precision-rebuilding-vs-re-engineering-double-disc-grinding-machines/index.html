Revitalizing Precision: Rebuilding Double Disc Grinding Machines

C&B compares two approaches to extending a double-disc grinder’s working life. Rebuilding restores the original or near-original mechanical condition; re-engineering changes the design, control or process capability to meet new requirements.

A rebuild focuses on worn parts, geometry and reliability. Re-engineering may introduce different feeds, automation, controls or tooling, with a wider scope and budget.

Choosing between them depends on the machine’s condition and the intended application: required quality, output, part changes and available capital. C&B’s article uses the example of an aging grinder with obsolete controls to frame this decision.
