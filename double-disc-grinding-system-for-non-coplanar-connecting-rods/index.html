Double Disc Grinding System for Non-Coplanar Connecting Rods

General Motors commissioned two C&B systems for coplanar and stepped connecting rods in the Chevy Volt’s auxiliary 1.4-liter engine. The turbo rod’s crank end was 1.2 mm narrower than its pin end.

The process removed 1.8 mm from the crank end and 0.6 mm from the pin end in one cycle, grinding all four faces at approximately 200 parts/hour. C&B reported average flatness and parallelism within 4 microns, repeating within 1 micron; size repeated within 3 microns, and the crank-to-pin step stayed within a 6-micron range.

Robotic unloading, camera orientation checking and automatic gauging inspected pin thickness, crank thickness and the step. The system used no hydraulics or disposable coolant-filter media. These are the historical program’s reported specifications and results.
