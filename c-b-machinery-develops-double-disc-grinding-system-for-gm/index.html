C&B Machinery Develops Double-Disc Grinding System for GM

GM’s Chevy Volt program required a stepped connecting rod to be ground in one setup. C&B developed a CNC double-disc process removing 1.8 mm at the crank end and 0.6 mm at the pin end.

The first 2010 machine reportedly achieved average flatness and parallelism within 4 microns, with 1-micron repeatability. Size repeated within 3 microns; the step relationship stayed within 6 microns. A robot transferred every part to automatic gauging, and the grinder corrected drift beyond preset limits.

The article reports 25% less horsepower than the compared conventional process. These measurements and energy claims describe the machine and application discussed at publication.
