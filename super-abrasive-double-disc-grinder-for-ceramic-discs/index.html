Super-Abrasive Double-Disc Grinder for Ceramic Discs

Island Ceramic Grinding in Gilroy, California, adopted a re-engineered CBV2-16-R/B for ceramic discs used in telecommunications and permanent magnets. Diamond grinding wheels, vertical opposed spindles and a rotary carrier supported the process. Runoff reached 300 parts/hour, twice the previous method’s output.

To meet the project budget, C&B retained and remanufactured mechanical feeds, replaced relay logic with a PLC and carefully balanced the spindles. Variable-frequency drives made wheel surface speed programmable, helping adapt the abrasive’s cutting behavior. Digital readouts enabled 0.000050-inch feed adjustments.

Small stock-removal increments and, where needed, multiple passes helped avoid damage to brittle parts. The project demonstrates a selective rebuild rather than a complete replacement of every subsystem.
