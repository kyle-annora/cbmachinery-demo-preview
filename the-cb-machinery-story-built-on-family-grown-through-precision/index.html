The C&B Machinery Story: Built on Family, Grown Through Precision

Joe Parker established C&B in 1980 and named the business for his daughters, Courtney and Brooke. Its early work refurbishing grinders developed the practical machine-building experience behind later new-equipment designs.

The September 2025 company story follows that progression from rebuilds to new precision grinding systems, with the business based in New Hudson, Michigan. It emphasizes the contributions of engineers, builders, machinists and service staff, and the continuing role of long-term customer relationships.
