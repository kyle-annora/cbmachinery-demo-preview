New Product: Clamp Bore Microfinishing Machine

C&B announced multiple orders for the CBV-3 clamp-bore microfinishing machine. The design addressed tighter face-to-bore perpendicularity and lead-variation requirements associated with transmission gear noise.

Each component is located and clamped on its internal diameter, then its faces are ground in separate, sequential operations. Multiple stations maintain throughput while permitting controlled stock removal on symmetrical and asymmetrical components.

The release describes a range of models and tooling arrangements to match part flow and automation, alongside a smaller footprint and lower operating costs than the compared methods. The CBV-3 is the model discussed in this historical announcement.
