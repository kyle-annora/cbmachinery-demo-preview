MFG Day 2018

C&B welcomed three busloads of Livingston County students for National Manufacturing Day 2018. The visit introduced students to the company’s machine-building facility as part of the Livingston Regional MFG Day program.

The program connected students with local employers including Hatch Stamping, Bekum, Thai Summit, Meritor, Flexible Metal and other area manufacturers and organizations. The event photographs document a past community visit.
