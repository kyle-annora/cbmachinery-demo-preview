Allison Transmission Purchases Fourth Cell from C & B Machinery

Allison Transmission ordered a fourth C&B grinding cell, bringing technology used in Chennai to its Indianapolis plant. The new cell was specified for three pinion configurations in the 3000-series transmission program, with provision for later part changes.

The rotary-plunge process introduces each gear through a carrier while both wheels remove stock. Parallel, concentric wheel settings support even wear and reduce dressing frequency. Every component receives gauge feedback. Earlier Allison cells reportedly exceeded 2.0 Ppk for overall height and parallelism.

Project figures: 15-second finished-part cycle; overall-height tolerance ±0.030 mm; parallelism 0.026 mm. These figures describe the reported Allison program.
