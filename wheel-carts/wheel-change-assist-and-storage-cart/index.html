Wheel Change Assist & Storage Cart

Published listing — confirm current availability, configuration and delivery with C&B. Sold equipment remains here as a reference.

Sizing

Standard model accommodates 23 to 36" grinding wheels, custom models can allow for 42" wheels.

Multi-Use

Functions with C & B supplied disc grinding wheels, as well as Besly, Gardner, Mattison and Giustina.

Safety

Engineered to address safety and ergonomic issues associated with changing grinding wheels.

Moving

All models are able to be towed behind in-plant vehicles and include the hitch to do so.

Wheel Change Cart Details

Manually operated and pneumatically lifted: There is a hand crank to rotate change flange into desired position. To raise/lower the table, there is a pneumatic lift to move wheel and plates into needed positions by the hand joystick controller mounted on the frame. This model comes with a tray to hold bolts and hand tools. This model will hold 4 grinding wheels and backing plates.
