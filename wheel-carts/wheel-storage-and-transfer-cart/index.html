Wheel Storage & Transfer Carts

Published listing — confirm current availability, configuration and delivery with C&B. Sold equipment remains here as a reference.

Sizes

Standard size cart holds up to 36" diameter wheels but carts can be customized to hold larger wheels

Quantity

Holds up to 4 grinding wheels and backing plates

No Damage

Each set of wheels is individually mounted to prevent nicks and chips

Moving

Tow hitch included that flips up out of the way when not in use

Specifications

  • Holds 4 grinding wheel and backing plate assemblies.
  • Removeable corner posts allow for easy loading and unloading.
  • Independently mounted, to prevent nicks and chips on the grinding wheels.
  • All models can be towed behind in-plant tug vehicles.
  • Store disc grinding wheels & backing plates up to 42" in diameter.
  • Custom solutions available for centerless grinding wheels with hubs.
