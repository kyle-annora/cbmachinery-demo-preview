Specialty Grinder for Large Non-Coplanar Connecting Rods

C&B received an order to remanufacture a Mattison 100C-5 for large connecting rods used in construction and mining equipment. The project retained a base machine while updating its control, feed and handling systems.

The design specified servo ball-screw feeds on five vertical spindles, rotary-table position tracking for robotic loading, a mobile touchscreen pendant and a five-axis Allen-Bradley safety-integrated control. Post-process gauging would feed size information back to the control.

Tooling accommodated two non-coplanar connecting rods without changeover. The source describes their length as over 21 inches (548 mm), with different crank/pin thicknesses and an asymmetrical pin. The figures reflect the original project announcement.
